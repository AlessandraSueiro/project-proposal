Publish Your Story with Mundo Books!

Our Deadlines

We accept manuscripts between September 1st and November 30th to ensure adequate time to read them all. In the month of December, we decide which projects to take on and contact writers about starting the publishing process which begins in January.

What We Are Looking For

As stated in our mission, we only publish literature by bilingual Latino authors. That being said, the contents of the novel do not need to necessarily focus on representing Latino culture and life. But, we prioritize language diversity and believe that an author’s background and life experiences influence their work. We have no genre focus beyond fiction as our focus is on the writer.

How Many Titles We Publish

Since simultaneously translating during the editing process doubles the work for us, we are able to publish four novels per year.
