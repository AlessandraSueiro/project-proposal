Our Mission

Mundo books strives to bring Latino literature to the United States by translating them in the process of composition. By publishing simultaneously more than one language edition, we are prioritizing the balance of cultural exchange. We believe that we can share our culture and our values through literature.

About Us

Mundo Books was founded in 2026 because we envisioned greater access to our own culture within the United States. Hollywood films are dubbed quickly, and American literature is translated quickly because it is consumed all over the world. We notice that the most politically powerful nations have a greater cultural impact. It is much rarer for Americans to consume dubbed films or translated literature, especially post-modern art. We desire to turn the tables and encourage translated literature within American culture.
